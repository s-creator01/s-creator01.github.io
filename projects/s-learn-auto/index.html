S Learn Auto.

A self-paced learning platform for industrial automation, with a structured roadmap, practical projects and study resources.

Made to be useful.

S Learn Auto helps learners move from electrical foundations toward industrial control systems through a structured, self-paced roadmap.

Visit website ↗

Explore the features

A learning roadmap

Follow 46 learning phases, starting with electrical fundamentals, electrical drawings and control devices, then progressing through motors, sensors, instrumentation, PLCs, HMI, drives, industrial networks, SCADA and commissioning.

Study and track progress

Explore phase checklists and progress tracking, use flashcard quizzes, and revisit the topics you want to practice. Interactive study tools require JavaScript.

Connect theory with practice

Browse practical automation project ideas and learning resources to connect concepts with simulations or training-lab practice. Review the project scope and equipment instructions before starting.

Who it is for

The roadmap is designed for self-paced study, whether you are beginning with electrical concepts or building knowledge of industrial control systems. It does not replace formal qualifications or equipment-specific instructions.
