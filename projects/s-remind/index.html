PROJECT / ANDROID

S Remind.

An offline Android app for reminders, notes, habits, Flow focus sessions, and privacy-focused tools.

Made to be useful.

S Remind brings reminders, notes, habits and focus sessions together in an Android app designed for offline use.

More details and release links will be shared when available.

Explore the features

Reminders and calendar

Browse a calendar and organize reminders into Today, Upcoming, Completed and Overdue views. Create reminders to keep track of everyday tasks.

Notes and Secure Vault

Keep notes together, browse and search them, and access a Secure Vault area intended for cards, passwords and private details.

Habits and progress

Track habits and personal goals, view streaks and journey or analytics screens, and record activity over time.

Flow focus sessions

Use a focus and break timer, keep quick tasks nearby, and see session counts and focus time for the day.

Milo assistant

Milo includes voice reminder and smart scanner options, along with shortcuts to tasks, notes and a timer. Milo is being improved: voice and scan results can contain mistakes and should be reviewed before saving.

Availability

S Remind is in development. The features described here are based on the current app preview; release details and download links will be added when available.
